Chapter 11: Protect Your Reputation

Be a Ratings All-Star

How do I manage what people say about my business?

Chapter Recap

Your reviews are the first thing new customers see. A strong rating wins trust before you say a word. This chapter helps you manage what people say. Reply to every review, good or bad, in a calm and genuine way. AI can draft those replies in seconds. You can also ask happy customers for reviews and watch for new ones. A few minutes a week keeps your reputation working for you.

Grab-and-Go Prompts

1. Respond to Any Review: works for positive, negative, or neutral

Write a professional, warm response to this [positive / negative / neutral] review for my [business type]. Acknowledge specifically what they said, thank them if appropriate, address any concerns raised, and invite them back. Keep it under 100 words. [paste review]

2. Turn a Message Into a Testimonial: get permission first

Format this customer message as a professional testimonial. Keep their words intact, tighten for length, and present it as a pull quote. [paste message]

3. Find the Gap Behind a Complaint: turn a bad review into a fix

What does this exchange suggest about a gap in my customer experience or communication? [paste the review and your response]

Your Move

Search your business name on Google right now. Read your most recent five reviews. If any are unanswered, use the response prompt from this chapter to draft and post a reply today. Then, if you haven’t already, set up a Google Alert for your business name. Finally, write one AI-generated review request message for a customer you served this week, and send it. These three actions take less than twenty minutes.
