Chapter 15: Your Industry. Your Advantage.

Find What Works for Your Business

I can see AI will work for them, but can it work for me, too?

Chapter Recap

You may wonder if AI really fits your kind of business. It does. This chapter gives ready-to-use prompts for common industries. Restaurants, retail, services, health, and real estate each get their own. Find your type and start there. If your industry is not listed, the chapter shows how to build your own prompts. The goal is simple: use AI where it helps your business most.

Grab-and-Go Prompts

1. Restaurant: Respond to a Review — works for any rating

You are a professional customer service writer. Write a professional, warm response to this review for my restaurant. Acknowledge what they said specifically, thank them if appropriate, and invite them back. [paste review]

2. Retail: Spot Product Trends — read your sales data

Which products are trending up? Which are slowing? Which are frequently bought together?

3. Any Industry: Find Your AI Fixes — start from your own time drains

You are a business efficiency expert. I run a [business type]. Here are my three biggest time drains: [list them]. For each one, suggest a specific AI tool or approach that could help. Keep it practical and beginner-friendly.

Your Move

Identify the three most time-consuming tasks specific to your business type. Write them down. Then ask AI: “You are a business efficiency expert. I run a [business type]. Here are my three biggest time drains: [list them]. For each one, suggest a specific AI tool or approach that could help. Keep it practical and beginner-friendly.”
