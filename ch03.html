Chapter 3: The Big Guys’ Secret Weapon

Now They’re in Your Crosshairs

How do I compete with competitors who have more resources?

Chapter Recap

For years, big companies had an edge you could not match. More money. More staff. More data. Their secret weapon turned out to be AI. Amazon uses it to guess what you want. Starbucks and Netflix use it to keep you coming back. This chapter shows how they do it. Then it shows how the same power is now in your hands, sized for a small business and your budget.

In This Chapter

  • How Starbucks Fills the Right Cups at the Right Time
  • How Netflix Keeps You Watching
  • What They Spent. What You Pay.
  • Your Small Business Superpower
  • A Lever Long Enough

Your Move

Ask AI this question right now: “I run a [describe your business]. Give me three specific ways I could use AI the way Amazon or Netflix uses it (personalization, prediction, or automation) scaled to my size and budget.” Write down the one idea that surprises you most. That is your first experiment.
