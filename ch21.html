Start Small. Win Big.

What can I do in 90 days?

Chapter Recap

Good intentions fade. A plan sticks. This chapter gives you a simple 90-day plan to put AI to work. The first week builds comfort. The first month gets one tool running. The next weeks add more and measure results. A weekly checklist keeps you on track. Small steps, done in order, add up fast. In 90 days you will have a real system, not just good intentions.
