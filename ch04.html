Chapter 4: You Have Concerns…

We Have Answers

I’m not the IT guy. Can I do this?

Chapter Recap

Think AI isn’t for you? You’re not alone. Most small business owners share the same five fears. Maybe you feel you’re not techy enough. Or you worry you’ll break something. Or lean on it too much. Or that customers will notice. Or that it takes too long to learn. None of these should stop you. AI is as easy as sending a text. Your first real win takes about ten minutes. The real risk isn’t trying AI. It’s waiting while other businesses race ahead.

In This Chapter

  • Five Fears. Five Answers.
  • The IT Guy Myth
  • AI Meets You Where You Are
  • Waiting Is the Real Risk

Your Move

Write down your single biggest fear about using AI. Just one sentence. Then write what it would mean for your business if that fear turned out to be completely unfounded. Keep both sentences somewhere you’ll see them. In 90 days, you will look back at the first sentence and barely recognize the person who wrote it.
